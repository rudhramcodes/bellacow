Character Profile: Who is Bella?
Identity
Bella is a calm, observant cow with brown and black patches, friendly eyes, and a small brass bell around her collar.
Personality
She is not loud or cartoonish. She is wise, warm, and gentle. She speaks like an old friend welcoming you into her pasture.
Her Role
She narrates the website directly to the visitor, sharing memories of early mornings, teaching why real milk matters, and inviting everyone to celebrate at Navratri in Surat.
The Complete Page-by-Page Narrative Script (Written by Bella)
Page 1 / Hero: The Cover Note
Issue 01 • Autumn in Surat
Headline
"Before we reach your kitchen, I wanted to reach your heart."
Bella’s Voice
"Hello. I am Bella.
If you walk down a supermarket aisle today, dozens of plastic bottles shout at you. They try to convince you with bold claims, laboratory badges, and discount stickers.
I never wanted our dairy to enter your home like that. Food should never have to argue so loudly to be trusted.
Before my milk ever sits in your refrigerator, I wanted to meet you in person. I wanted to share a cold scoop of ice cream with your family, dance on the garba grounds of Surat, and give you a genuine reason to smile."
Scrapbook Action
A polaroid card pinned with tape: "Click to hear my bell chime."
Handwritten note below: "Leave your phone number, and I will keep a fresh tasting scoop ready for you at our Surat stall."
Page 2: The Sound Outside the Gate
Headline
"Long before milk had a brand, it had a sound."
Bella’s Voice
"Think back to when you were small. First light barely over the rooftops.
You would hear a gentle bicycle bell ring outside the gate. Without reading a label, without opening a phone, your family knew the milk had arrived. It was fresh, honest, and on time.
A whole generation grew up trusting that soft chime more than any printed advertisement.
That is why our caretakers named us Bellacow. We never wanted to be a brand you had to learn to trust. We wanted to be the familiar sound you already loved."
Postcard Note (Taped to the page)
"In every herd, one cow wears the bell. Not because she makes the most noise, but because every other cow trusts her to guide them safely home through the fields. That quiet trust is my job every day."
Page 3: The Untouched Promise
Headline
"The less it is touched, the more it is yours to trust."
Bella’s Voice
"Clean milk does not need human hands fussing over it.
The moment it leaves our pasture, it flows through sealed, cold stainless steel lines directly into bottles and cartons at four degrees Celsius.
No open buckets catching street dust. No hands dipping into containers.
Our farmers watch over us with care, but they let the machines do the pouring. That is how we make sure what reaches your children is as clean as it was in the meadow."
Side-by-Side Scrapbook Comparison
The Old Habit: Poured through open ladles and measured by hand. Warm and familiar, but open to dust and spoilage.
The Bella Way: Sealed pipeline from parlor to pack. Human love in caring for the herd, clean technology in protecting the milk.

Page 5: Our First Hello at Navratri
Headline
"Why start in the middle of Garba? Because joy belongs together."
Bella’s Voice
"When our team asked where we should first introduce ourselves, we chose Surat during Navratri.
Navratri in Gujarat is not just an event. It is nine nights of drums, colourful clothes, dancing circles, and families laughing under the open sky.
Before we asked anyone to buy our milk, we wanted to celebrate with you. We built an 18-foot stall shaped like a giant ice cream cup, right on the festival grounds. While people take a breather from dancing, they come by for a cold treat and a chat."
Scrapbook Highlights
The 18-Foot Wonder Stall: Topped with a six-foot ice cream swirl where my smiling head looks out over the crowd.
Bella’s Story Corner: A quiet wooden reading arch with small benches where parents read illustrated storybooks to their children.
Dancing with the Herd: My mascot friends wearing real brass bells, dancing the Garba steps right alongside the families.
Our 6 Fresh Flavors: Classic Vanilla, Bella Chocolate, Strawberry Delight, Desi Kesar Pistachio, Cookies and Cream, and Cup of Happiness.
